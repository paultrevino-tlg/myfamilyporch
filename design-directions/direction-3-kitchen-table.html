Why now

There are stories we always mean to ask about.

How they met. The house they grew up in. The job nobody talks about anymore. We tell ourselves we’ll sit down and ask one day — and then the chance quietly slips away.

My Family Porch is that conversation, made easy enough to actually happen, and saved before it’s gone.

How it works

Three simple steps.

you do the easy setup. they just answer the phone. we keep the stories.

1

Set it up in minutes

Add your loved one and choose the topics you’d love to hear about. No app for them to install — it works over a normal phone.

2

Why voice

A transcript can’t laugh. Their voice can.

“…and your grandfather walked the whole way home in the rain, just so he could say he’d done it.”

The way they pause, the catch in their throat at the good part, the way they say your name — that’s the part you’ll miss most. We keep the real recording, not just the words.

  • ✓It’s their actual voice — saved, not summarized.
  • ✓Nothing to learn: answer a prompt out loud, that’s it.
  • ✓Works for the least tech-comfortable elder in the family.

FAQ

Questions families ask.

Does the storyteller need a smartphone or app?

No. It works over a normal phone with SMS. There is nothing to install, no account for them to manage, and no password to remember.

Can more than one person record?

Yes, on the Family plan, or add a storyteller to any annual plan.

What are the voice QR codes?

Printed codes in the book that play the original recording when scanned, so you can hear the story in their own voice.

Can I download all the recordings?

Yes — any time, on every plan. One click gives you a ZIP with every audio recording, the transcripts, and your book. If you ever cancel, it’s all still yours to download and keep.

Can I order more printed copies?

Yes, any time, softcover or hardcover.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep all stories, audio, and your digital book. Nothing is deleted or locked.

A letter from the founder

It started with a Father’s Day gift I couldn’t find.

My Family Porch began at home, on a Sunday morning, with one simple wish: that my dad could just tell us the stories of his life.

He has a cell phone, and he knows how to use it. So why not make sharing his memories as easy as talking on the phone? And you don’t have to keep paying us forever to hold onto your loved one’s memories — they’re yours to keep.

I love you, Dad.

Paul — Founder, My Family Porch
